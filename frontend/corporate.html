QUESTIONS

Corporate Client Inquiries

What is the minimum order quantity?

For dedicated corporate commissions with bespoke packaging and custom branding, our minimum order quantity is 50 hampers.

Why is there a 3-month lead time?

We do not stock pre-made catalog goods. We source artisanal items fresh, mill custom luxury boxes, and foil-stamp your ribbons to guarantee pristine exclusivity.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. Complete GST-compliant institutional invoices with full input tax credit breakdown are provided for all corporate clients.

Can you ship individually to employee addresses?

Yes. You provide the address spreadsheet and our white-glove courier partners handle direct doorstep delivery nationwide with active tracking.
