QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can packaging match our wedding invite theme?

Absolutely. Send us a swatch or digital copy of your wedding card, and our atelier will match fabrics, ribbon hues, and foiling accents to your palette.

Can you cater to dietary preferences?

Yes. We source purely vegetarian, gluten-free, sugar-free, or Jain-compliant sweets and confections upon your specification.

Can hampers be delivered directly to our venue?

Yes. We deliver directly to banquet halls, farmhouses, and destination wedding hotels across India on the exact date requested.

Can we inspect a prototype hamper first?

Yes. Once your order concept and deposit are confirmed, we create and share detailed high-resolution video and photo prototypes before batch assembly.
