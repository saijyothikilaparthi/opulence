ABOUT OPULENCE

A More Personal Way to Gift

At Opulence, gifting begins with understanding the person, occasion, and intention behind every gift. We bring together carefully selected products, thoughtful presentation, personalised details, and considered packaging to create hampers that feel meaningful to the people receiving them.

From corporate gifting to personal celebrations, every hamper is planned around your preferences and budget. We reject cookie-cutter catalogs in favour of authentic collaboration—ensuring each artifact carries warmth, elegance, and purpose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning bespoke hampers with Opulence.

Unlike off-the-shelf gift baskets, every Opulence hamper is created around the recipient, occasion, theme, and your budget. We curate individual products, design customized packaging and ribbons, and handwrite or print bespoke message cards tailored specifically for your order.
Yes. You can select specific items from our recommended categories (coffee, teas, fine confections, lifestyle artifacts, brassware) or request unique items. You can also pick from rigid gift boxes, woven cane baskets, handcrafted wooden trunks, or sustainable kraft packaging.
