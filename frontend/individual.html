QUESTIONS

Individual Gifting Questions

Can I order just 1 single hamper?

Yes! The Individual Collection is crafted specifically for 1-of-1 private commissions, with zero minimum order quantities.

Can I include my own personal letter or gift item?

Yes. You can send us a personal gift or heirloom, or provide text for our atelier calligrapher to hand-inscribe in archival ink.

How quickly can a single hamper be crafted?

While we prefer 2-3 weeks for bespoke sourcing, priority curations can often be prepared in 5-7 business days depending on item availability.

Is discreet surprise delivery available?

Yes. We coordinate directly with you so the hamper arrives as an elegant surprise at their doorstep, with no invoice enclosed in the box.

Opulence is a bespoke gifting studio. Every commission is designed around the recipient, occasion, and brand identity. No two hampers are ever alike.
